# IEEE 802.11 frame analysis

Apply this reference only after identifying the protocol and export semantics. Do not infer all capabilities from an 802.11ax label.

## Eligibility and direction

Identify transmitter/receiver addresses, traffic direction relative to the experiment, and frame subtype. Source/destination labels can differ from TA/RA in bridged or multi-address frames. Separate QoS data, non-QoS data, management and control populations. Use decoded roles where possible; do not aggregate every frame's PHY rate into a user-data rate.

Keep MCS, bandwidth/RU allocation, GI, NSS, NSTS, PHY format and coding interpretation distinct. NSTS is not universally NSS; STBC and format-specific signaling matter. Parse exported strings and hexadecimal values with explicit rules. Compare only compatible PHY configurations, or present them as part of the mechanism under investigation.

## Retries and sequence spaces

Reconstruct observed attempt chains within capture/interface/channel/link, TA, RA, TID and fragment context. Account for the 12-bit sequence space, reuse, capture gaps, retry flags, time ordering and plausible packet lifetime. Deduplicate duplicate observations only with defensible capture identities. Sequence wrap is not packet loss or a device reset.

Without TID, multiple QoS queues may reuse the same sequence number. Do not merge them into one chain. Without evidence of the first attempt, mark left censoring; without ACK/final outcome or known lifetime completion, mark right censoring. `tools/wifi_frames.py` intentionally reports censored observed chains.

An observed maximum chain length is affected by capture duration, load, traffic class, PHY rates, missing frames and stopping policies. Report tails, duration and attempted-rate sequences. Claim a configured retry limit only with configuration/firmware evidence or a suitable controlled experiment. A long retry chain may preserve delivery while increasing delay; link its burden to the observed symptom.

## A-MPDU reference validation

Radiotap's reference number is generated by the capture device. It is intended to be shared across subframes of an aggregate and is not globally unique across channels [1]. An anomaly in it may be a capture/export behavior, not the transmitting vendor's aggregation policy.

Check within-capture/channel scope, repeated references, temporal locality, endpoint consistency, sequence relationships, last-subframe flags and available PPDU boundaries. A nearly unique reference for every observed MPDU does not prove aggregation is absent, and repeated references alone do not prove trustworthy aggregate boundaries.

Prefer validated PPDU/aggregate metadata. When unavailable, preserve uncertain grouping rather than manufacturing exact aggregate counts.

## Block Ack reasoning

Decode the BA variant, addressing, TID, starting sequence and bitmap using verified byte/bit order. Compressed single-TID 64/256-bit decoding is available as a primitive; Basic BA, Multi-TID, Multi-STA and unsupported extensions require separate tested decoders. Do not truncate an unsupported bitmap to make it fit.

Map bits to the correct sequence space modulo 4096. Match only observed eligible transmissions in the appropriate receiver window and exchange context. Maintain acknowledgment state across retries. A zero bit is not automatically a final failure; unused positions are not attempted packets. `observed_ba_status` reports only the supplied observed sequence numbers and does not itself associate frames.

BA followed by RTS/CTS can help delimit an **exchange candidate**. It does not universally establish exactly one A-MPDU: TXOPs, multiple aggregates, delayed/cumulative acknowledgment and missing captures can break that inference. Label heuristic exchange boundaries and validate against a capture with trustworthy PPDU metadata before treating them as aggregate truth.

## Useful mechanism-specific views

Use one or two targeted figures per finding: retry-chain tail versus outcome; PHY-mode transitions and response delay; observed transmission burden during a throughput deficit; validated aggregate-size distribution; BA acknowledgment coverage and missing evidence. State frame population, direction, censoring and source rows in captions.
